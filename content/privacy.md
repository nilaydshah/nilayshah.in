This website uses **Google Analytics 4** to understand aggregate traffic and improve the site.

Analytics may include information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, referring website, and engagement with the site. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to provide these insights.

Analytics storage is denied by default. If you select **Accept analytics**, the site updates Google Consent Mode and permits analytics storage. If you select **Reject**, analytics storage remains denied. Advertising storage and ad personalization remain denied in either case.

When analytics storage is denied, Google Consent Mode may send cookieless pings containing coarse information, but it does not read or write analytics cookies.

Your choice is saved in your browser using local storage under `nilayshah-analytics-consent`. You can change it at any time through **Cookie settings** in the site footer.

I use aggregate information to understand which content is useful and how visitors discover the website. I do not use the site to sell personal information or serve personalized advertising.

You can learn more in [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and use the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) if you prefer not to participate.

Links to services such as Substack, LinkedIn, X, Instagram, and GitHub take you to external websites with their own privacy practices.

This notice may be updated as the website and its analytics evolve.
