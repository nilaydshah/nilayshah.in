I am **Nilay Shah**, an Azure Storage engineering leader at Microsoft. I lead the Control Plane organization behind foundational Azure storage services.

The platform powers the lifecycle of services such as Azure Storage accounts, managed disks, and Elastic SAN. At that scale, engineering is about more than individual features. It is about designing distributed systems that evolve safely, creating platforms that make teams effective, and building an operating model that can keep improving.

## What I work on

My work sits at the intersection of three systems:

**The cloud platform.** Building and operating massive-scale Control Plane services where availability, consistency, security, and safe change all matter.

**The system of engineering.** Improving developer experience, platform capabilities, operational workflows, and the feedback loops that help distributed teams move with greater clarity and efficiency.

**The AI-native team.** Bringing agentic systems into real engineering work—from development and incident response to knowledge, automation, and decision support—while preserving governance and human accountability.

I am especially interested in what happens after the proof of concept: how a promising idea earns trust, becomes measurable, integrates with existing systems, and reaches production responsibly.

## How I think about leadership

A platform and a team are both systems. Their outcomes are shaped by architecture, interfaces, incentives, feedback loops, and the quality of decisions made over time.

My role is not only to deliver technology. It is to create the conditions in which teams across geographies can understand the mission, make good decisions, learn from evidence, and continually improve how they build and operate.

That is also why AI-native transformation interests me. The opportunity is not simply to make individual tasks faster. It is to redesign the system of engineering so people can spend more time on judgment, creativity, and customer impact.

## The AI-Native Engineer

I publish **[The AI-Native Engineer](https://theainativeengineer.substack.com/)**, where I share field notes, case studies, and lessons on how AI is changing the way engineers build, operate, and lead.

The goal is to move beyond demos and hype and focus on the practical questions:

- What works in real engineering environments?
- What breaks as an idea moves toward production?
- How do teams measure quality and build trust?
- Where should AI act, and where should people remain firmly in control?
- How do leaders improve engineering efficiency without losing reliability or accountability?
